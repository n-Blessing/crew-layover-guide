Africa/Accra

Accra, served by Kotoka International (ACC). A likely crew hotel area is Airport City, about a 10-minute drive from the terminal, with properties such as Accra Marriott, Four Points by Sheraton Accra Kotoka Airport and The Palms. Currency is the Ghanaian cedi (GHS). Tips here focus on food and things to do close to that airport area.

Food

Eating on a short stop

  • Café Kwae in Airport City

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    Café Kwae is on the ground floor of One Airport Square, right in Airport City, so you can walk or take a very short ride from the airport hotels.

    It's a casual cafe for breakfast or a simple meal without leaving the area. Check the opening times before you go.

  • Crew favourite: Buka Restaurant in Osu

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Moderate

    When I want actual Ghanaian food, I take a short taxi to Buka on 10th Street in Osu, usually under 20 minutes from the airport area in normal traffic.

    It's a well-known spot for West African dishes. Check they're open that day, and note that they close on Sundays.

  • Vida e Caffè at One Airport Square

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    Vida e Caffè has a branch in the same One Airport Square building, handy for coffee and a light bite when you're staying in Airport City.

    It's one of the closest food stops to the Kotoka hotel cluster.

Sightseeing

What can fit in under a day

  • A walk at Labadi Beach

    Time
    Under 3 hours
    Cost
    Low

    Labadi Beach is Accra's best-known public beach, usually about 20 minutes by taxi from the airport area.

    I'll go for a walk on the sand or a drink at a beach bar, and I keep an eye on the time so I can get back for report.

  • Makola Market for fabrics and street life

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Low

    Makola Market is Accra's main open-air market, typically a 15- to 20-minute ride from Airport City.

    It's the place for fabrics, beads and street energy, and I only go if I have a clear way back to the hotel afterwards.

  • Shop at Accra Mall

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Low

    Accra Mall sits by the Tetteh Quarshie Interchange, only a few kilometres and a short drive from Airport City and Kotoka.

    This is my go-to mall on an Accra layover: shops, food and a cinema, and I'm back at the hotel quickly.
