Africa/Johannesburg

Johannesburg, served by O.R. Tambo International (JNB). A likely crew hotel area is the airport precinct itself, including InterContinental Johannesburg O.R. Tambo, linked by a covered walkway from international arrivals, and City Lodge Hotel at O.R. Tambo, reached indoors via Parkade 2. Currency is the South African rand (ZAR). Tips here focus on food and things to do close to that airport area.

Food

Eating on a short stop

  • Crew favourite: Nando's at Emperors Palace

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    If you're staying in the OR Tambo precinct, Emperors Palace is a few minutes away and has a Nando's, which is an easy, familiar, affordable meal.

    The complex is close enough that you can eat and get back without a long transfer.

  • Ocean Basket at Emperors Palace

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Moderate

    Ocean Basket at Emperors Palace is a popular seafood and sushi chain if you want a sit-down meal near the airport.

    It's still in the same close precinct, so you don't have to go into Sandton or the city.

  • Spur at Emperors Palace

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    Spur at Emperors Palace is a casual family restaurant with generous portions, another straightforward option a short ride from the airport hotels.

    Confirm it's open, then you're only a few kilometres from the terminal.

Sightseeing

What can fit in under a day

  • A short outing to Emperors Palace

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Low

    Emperors Palace is about 2.7 km from the airport hotels and advertises an airport shuttle; check the current shuttle times if you plan to use it.

    Even if you're not staying there, it's a handy nearby spot for food or a short walk around the complex that still keeps you close to report.

  • Shop at Festival Mall in Kempton Park

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Low

    Festival Mall in Kempton Park is the closest proper mall to OR Tambo, a short drive from the airport hotels, with more than 150 stores and an ice rink.

    I'll go here for a simple shopping outing and stay on this side of town.
