Dubai, served by Dubai International (DXB). A likely crew hotel area near the airport is Al Garhoud, a few minutes from the terminal, with properties such as Premier Inn Dubai International Airport. Currency is the UAE dirham (AED). Tips here focus on food and things to do close to that airport area.

Food

Eating on a short stop

  • Abo Ali Restaurant & Cafe in Al Garhoud

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    Abo Ali is another easy option in Al Garhoud, close to the airport hotels, with Lebanese and Middle Eastern dishes in a casual cafe setting.

    I use this kind of place when I want a proper meal without a long ride back. Check it's still serving before you go.

  • Crew favourite: Pak Darbar in Al Garhoud

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    If you're staying in the Al Garhoud airport area, Pak Darbar is a popular, affordable Pakistani restaurant close to the hotel.

    It's a straightforward stop for a filling meal when you don't want to go into the city. Confirm it's open before you head out.

  • The Irish Village, a short hop from the hotel

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Moderate

    The Irish Village sits in Al Garhoud next to the tennis stadium, a short hop from the airport hotels.

    It's a well-known spot for a relaxed meal or a drink outdoors, and you stay close enough to get back for report.

Sightseeing

What can fit in under a day

  • Dubai Festival City Mall and the IMAGINE show

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Low

    Dubai Festival City Mall is about ten minutes from DXB and has a waterfront promenade plus the IMAGINE light and fountain show.

    It's an easy outing from the Al Garhoud hotel area when you want a mall and something to look at, not just the shops.

  • Shop at City Centre Deira

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Low

    City Centre Deira is the closest big mall to DXB, only a few minutes from the Al Garhoud hotels.

    On a short layover I'll go here for shops, a bite, or VOX Cinemas, and I'm still close enough to the airport.
