Europe/Brussels

Brussels, served by Brussels Airport (BRU) in Zaventem. A likely crew hotel area is right at the airport, including Sheraton Brussels Airport Hotel, just steps from the terminal, and Crowne Plaza Brussels Airport, about 2.5 km away with a shuttle. Currency is the euro (EUR). Tips here focus on food and things to do close to that airport area.

Food

Eating on a short stop

  • Crew favourite: 'T Gouden Ambacht in Zaventem

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Moderate

    'T Gouden Ambacht is a Belgian-French brasserie on Heldenplein in the centre of Zaventem, a short ride from the airport hotels.

    It's a popular local spot for a proper dinner close to the airport, so you don't have to go into Brussels just to eat. Confirm they're open that evening.

  • Restaurant Anton Pieck, close to the airport

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Moderate

    Anton Pieck on Vilvoordelaan calls itself the closest restaurant to Brussels Airport, and it's been serving Belgian-French food since 1981.

    If your hotel is at the airport or in Zaventem, this is a short hop for a sit-down meal. Check the opening days before you go, as it is not a seven-day operation.

Sightseeing

What can fit in under a day

  • Grand-Place on the airport train

    Time
    Under 3 hours
    Cost
    Low

    The airport train sits under the terminal and gets you to Brussels-Central in around 18 minutes, and from there Grand-Place is a short walk.

    It's the easiest city outing I make from a BRU airport hotel, and I still leave plenty of time for the train back.

  • Shop at Woluwe Shopping

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Low

    Woluwe Shopping is a large mall in Woluwe-Saint-Lambert, about 9 km from the airport and typically a short drive from the Zaventem hotel area.

    I'll go here when I want shops and a food court without riding all the way into the city centre.
