Asia/Qatar

Doha, served by Hamad International (DOH). A likely crew hotel area near the airport is the Old Airport and Business Park stretch, about a 15-minute drive landside, with properties such as Hyatt Regency Oryx Doha and Crowne Plaza Doha - The Business Park. Currency is the Qatari riyal (QAR). Tips here focus on food and things to do close to that airport area.

Food

Eating on a short stop

  • Bawarchi Restaurant for biryani near the hotel

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    Bawarchi Restaurant, also on Matar Qadeem Street, is known for Hyderabadi dishes and biryani at reasonable prices.

    It's a solid sit-down option if you're staying in the airport-area hotels and want a proper plate of food.

  • Crew favourite: Habeeb Restaurant on Matar Qadeem

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    If your hotel is in the Old Airport or Business Park area, Habeeb Restaurant on Matar Qadeem Street is a local favourite for affordable Turkish food.

    It's close enough that you don't have to go into town for a decent meal. Check it's open before you set out.

  • Mashawi Al Arabi for a quick shawarma

    Time
    About 1 hour
    Cost
    Low

    Mashawi Al Arabi is a popular shawarma stop on the same Old Airport stretch, handy when you want something quick and cheap near the hotel.

    Confirm it's open, then you can be back with time to spare.

Sightseeing

What can fit in under a day

  • Shop at City Center Doha

    Time
    Under 2 hours
    Cost
    Low

    City Center Doha in West Bay is a large mall with shops, dining and a cinema, and it's generally within about a 20-minute drive of Hamad International.

    I treat this as the easy mall outing from an airport-area hotel when I don't want to go far.

  • Walk Souq Waqif on a short outing

    Time
    Under 3 hours
    Cost
    Low

    Souq Waqif is Doha's old market, usually around a 15- to 20-minute drive from the airport.

    I'll walk the lanes, look at spices and textiles, and eat something simple, then head back before the evening rush.
